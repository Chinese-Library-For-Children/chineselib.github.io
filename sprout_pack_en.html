《伴我长大经典童话》

这些跨越了几十年的故事，
曾照亮过我们的双眼，
现在，轮到我们讲给孩子听了。

在异国他乡的灯下，这些故事不只是语言的学习，更是血脉里的共鸣。
孩子读到了勇敢与智慧，而我们，在阅读中重逢了那个小时候的自己。

这是一份跨越山海的礼物，也是两代人之间最温柔的共同语言。

Why Families Love Sprout Pack

  • 🌏   Discover Chinese culture through stories
    • Children experience traditions, humor, family life, and different ways of seeing the world through Chinese children’s literature.
  • 🎧   Chinese becomes part of story time
    • As children listen to the story in English, they naturally hear and begin recognizing a few simple Chinese words—no lessons or Chinese-speaking adult required.
  • 🏡   Easy for the whole family to enjoy
    • No Chinese background is needed. Parents and children can explore the story, language, and culture together.
  • 🌱   Small steps build lasting connections
    • One carefully selected book each season helps children grow more familiar with Chinese language and culture over time.
